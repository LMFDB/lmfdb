Given a map $f\colon X_G\to X_H$ of {{KNOWL('shimcurve','Shimura curves')}} induced by an inclusion of open compact subgroups $H\le G\le \Aut_{\pm\mu}(O)\ltimes\widehat{O}^\times$, the **relative index** of $f$ is the index $[G:H]$ in the {{KNOWL('shimcurve.enhanced_group','enhanced group')}}. The {{KNOWL('shimcurve.modular_cover','degree')}} of $f$ is half the relative index when $(1,-1)\in G$ and $(1,-1)\notin H$, and equals the relative index otherwise.
