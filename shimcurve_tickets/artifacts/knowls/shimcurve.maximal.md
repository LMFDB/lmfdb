Let $B$ be a {{KNOWL('shimcurve.quaternion_algebra','quaternion algebra')}} over $\Q$. An {{KNOWL('shimcurve.order','order')}} $O \subset B$ is **maximal** if it is not properly contained in any other order of $B$.

Every order of $B$ is contained in a maximal order, but&mdash;unlike for {{KNOWL('nf.order','orders in number fields')}}&mdash;a rational quaternion algebra never has a unique maximal order. The number of $B^\times$-conjugacy classes of maximal orders is finite; for the indefinite division algebras relevant to {{KNOWL('shimcurve','Shimura curves')}} there is exactly one such class, and its {{KNOWL('shimcurve.disco','reduced discriminant')}} equals the {{KNOWL('shimcurve.discb','discriminant')}} $D$ of $B$.
