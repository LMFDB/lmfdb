The **$K$-gonality** $\gamma$ of a {{KNOWL('shimcurve', 'Shimura curve')}} $X_H$ is the {{KNOWL('ag.gonality','gonality')}} of any of its {{KNOWL('ag.geom_component', 'geometric components')}} over $K$.  (The geometric gonality of that component might be smaller than the $K$-gonality.)
