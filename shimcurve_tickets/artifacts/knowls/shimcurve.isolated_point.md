A closed point $x$ on a {{KNOWL('shimcurve','Shimura curve')}} $X_H$ is **isolated** if it is an {{KNOWL('ag.isolated_point','isolated point')}}: it does not belong to an infinite family of effective divisors of its degree parametrized by $\mathbb{P}^1$ or by a positive-rank abelian variety. Isolated points are of particular interest because, in contrast to non-isolated points, they cannot be produced by the two systematic sources of low-degree points (maps to $\mathbb{P}^1$ and positive-rank subvarieties of the Jacobian).
