For hyperelliptic {{KNOWL('shimcurve','Shimura curves')}} and curves of genus less than $2$, the {{KNOWL('ag.canonical_model','canonical map')}} is not an embedding. For such curves $X_H$ we take the lowest even weight $k$ for which Riemann–Roch guarantees an embedding into the projectivization of the corresponding space of quaternionic modular forms of weight $k$. The image of this embedding is the **embedded model** of $X_H$.
