A **plane model** of a {{KNOWL('shimcurve','Shimura curve')}} $X_H$ is a (possibly singular) model of $X_H$ as a plane curve in $\mathbb{P}^2$; see {{KNOWL('ag.plane_model','plane model')}}. Plane models are obtained by projecting the {{KNOWL('ag.canonical_model','canonical')}} or {{KNOWL('shimcurve.embedded_model','embedded model')}} of $X_H$ from a suitable linear subspace, or from a gonal map to $\mathbb{P}^1$ exhibiting the {{KNOWL('shimcurve.gonality','gonality')}} of $X_H$.
