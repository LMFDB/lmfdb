The **$j$-invariant map** of a {{KNOWL('shimcurve','Shimura curve')}} $X_H$ is the forgetful morphism $X_H \to X_G$ to the level-one base curve $G=\Aut_{\pm\mu}(O)\ltimes\widehat{O}^\times$, which sends a {{KNOWL('shimcurve.pqm','PQM')}} abelian surface with {{KNOWL('shimcurve.level_structure','$H$-level structure')}} to the underlying surface. It is the analogue, for Shimura curves, of the {{KNOWL('modcurve','modular curve')}} map $j\colon X_H\to X(1)$. When the base curve has {{KNOWL('shimcurve.genus','genus')}} $0$ with a chosen coordinate $\cong\mathbb{P}^1$, this map realizes $X_H$ as a cover of $\mathbb{P}^1$ and is presented by giving that coordinate as a rational function on the displayed {{KNOWL('shimcurve.model','model')}}.
