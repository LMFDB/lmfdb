title: Residue field of a point on a Shimura curve

The **residue field** of a closed point $x$ on a {{KNOWL('shimcurve','Shimura curve')}} $X$ defined over a field $K$ is the field $K(x)$. Its degree over $K$ is the {{KNOWL('shimcurve.point_degree','degree')}} of $x$.
