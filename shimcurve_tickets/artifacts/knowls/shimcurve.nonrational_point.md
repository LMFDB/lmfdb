A **non-rational low-degree point** on a {{KNOWL('shimcurve','Shimura curve')}} $X_H$ over $\Q$ is a closed point $x$ of {{KNOWL('shimcurve.point_degree','degree')}} $[\Q(x):\Q]>1$ that is small enough to have been found and recorded. Such points correspond to {{KNOWL('shimcurve.pqm','PQM')}} abelian surfaces defined over the {{KNOWL('shimcurve.point_residue_field','residue field')}} $\Q(x)$ but not over $\Q$. Each recorded point is annotated with its degree, residue field, whether it has {{KNOWL('ag.complex_multiplication','complex multiplication')}}, and whether it is {{KNOWL('shimcurve.isolated_point','isolated')}}.
